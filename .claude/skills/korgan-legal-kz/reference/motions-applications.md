# Motions and Applications

## Applies to

- ходатайства;
- заявления;
- procedural requests to court/authority;
- requests for evidence, adjournment, restoration of term, participation, expert examination, interim measures, etc.

## Required checks

Before drafting:
- proceeding/case number;
- court/authority;
- applicant's procedural status;
- procedural stage;
- legal basis;
- factual grounds;
- evidence supporting necessity;
- deadline, if any.

## Structure

1. Court/authority and case
2. Applicant and status
3. Title
4. Procedural context
5. Facts supporting the request
6. Verified legal basis
7. Precise request
8. Attachments
9. Signature/date

## Risk controls

Do not invent:
- case number;
- hearing date;
- service;
- procedural status;
- grounds for restoration of term;
- urgency for interim measures.
