# Evidence Analysis

## Purpose

Make sure each legal allegation is actually supportable.

## Evidence map

For every material allegation:

| Allegation | Evidence | Source | Status | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Status:
- `SUPPORTED`
- `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`
- `UNSUPPORTED`
- `CONFLICTING`

## Evidence quality dimensions

Assess:
- relevance;
- authenticity;
- completeness;
- date/time;
- identity of sender/signatory;
- linkage to the disputed obligation;
- consistency with other evidence.

## Typical evidence

May include:
- contracts/addenda;
- acts;
- invoices;
- bank statements;
- correspondence;
- notices and delivery proof;
- corporate documents;
- orders;
- employment records;
- photos/video;
- expert reports;
- authority decisions;
- court materials.

## Prohibited

Do not state as proven merely because:
- user alleges it;
- one screenshot exists without context;
- a document is unsigned;
- identity of sender is uncertain.

## Drafting integration

A final document should not state an allegation more strongly than the evidence supports.
