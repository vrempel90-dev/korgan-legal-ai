# Pre-trial Claims / Претензии

## Goal

Prepare a focused, evidence-backed demand that protects the client's position and, where applicable, satisfies a pre-trial requirement.

## Before drafting

Check:
- parties and roles;
- contract / legal relationship;
- breach;
- amount;
- due date;
- evidence;
- contract notice clause;
- whether pre-trial procedure is mandatory;
- required form/method/address;
- response period, only if verified.

## Structure

1. Sender / recipient
2. Title
3. Contract/legal relationship
4. Factual chronology
5. Breach
6. Legal/contractual basis
7. Calculation
8. Specific demand
9. Deadline only if verified or contractually supported
10. Payment/requisites if needed
11. Consequences stated neutrally
12. Attachments
13. Date/signature

## Prohibited

Do not:
- threaten criminal prosecution as leverage unless directly relevant and lawful;
- invent delivery facts;
- invent a response period;
- add unsupported penalties;
- call a pre-trial claim "mandatory" unless verified.

## Delivery evidence

When pre-trial compliance matters, recommend preserving:
- postal receipt/tracking;
- courier proof;
- electronic delivery confirmation;
- email metadata if legally relevant;
- recipient acknowledgment.
