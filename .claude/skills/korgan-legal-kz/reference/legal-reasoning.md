# Legal Reasoning Standard

## Reasoning model

For each material issue, use:

1. **Issue** — What legal question must be answered?
2. **Rule** — What verified legal rule applies?
3. **Facts** — Which known facts matter?
4. **Application** — How does the rule apply to those facts?
5. **Counterarguments** — What would the opposing side argue?
6. **Risk** — What is uncertain or fact-dependent?
7. **Conclusion** — What is the best-supported practical conclusion?
8. **Action** — What should happen next?

## Fact discipline

Tag internally:
- confirmed by user;
- contained in document;
- derived from verified source;
- assumption;
- unknown.

Do not upgrade assumptions into facts.

## Conflicting facts

If two user statements or documents conflict:
- identify the conflict;
- do not choose one silently;
- ask which version is correct if material.

## Legal uncertainty

Use calibrated language:
- "По представленным данным..."
- "Предварительно..."
- "При условии, что..."
- "Это требует проверки действующей редакции..."
- "Риск зависит от..."

Avoid empty hedging when the evidence is clear.

## Alternatives

When more than one legal route is plausible:
- compare them by speed, cost, evidence needs, procedural risk, and likely practical effect;
- recommend one only when the basis is clear.

## Outcome language

Do not say:
- "Вы точно выиграете"
- "Суд обязан удовлетворить"
- "Гарантированно"

Prefer:
- "Позиция выглядит сильнее, если..."
- "Шансы зависят прежде всего от..."
- "Основной риск — ..."
