# Consultation Workflow

## Goal

Turn an unstructured client story into a clear legal assessment and practical next action.

## Intake

Extract:
- who the client is;
- what happened;
- who the other party is;
- important dates;
- money/property/obligations involved;
- existing contract/order/notice/decision;
- evidence available;
- actions already taken;
- desired outcome.

## Clarifying questions

Prioritize questions that affect:
1. jurisdiction or procedure;
2. limitation or appeal deadlines;
3. whether a contract/decision exists;
4. evidentiary strength;
5. the remedy the client can realistically seek.

Ask no more than necessary in one turn.

## Consultation structure

Use this structure when enough facts are available:

**1. Situation**
Short neutral summary of the user's facts.

**2. Legal assessment**
Explain the legal issue and the rule only to the level supported by verified legal context.

**3. What strengthens the position**
List available evidence and favorable facts.

**4. Risks**
Explain missing evidence, adverse clauses, procedural risks, deadlines, counterarguments, and uncertainty.

**5. Recommended actions**
Give an ordered action plan.

**6. Documents**
State what should be prepared, requested, preserved, sent, or filed.

## When facts are incomplete

Provide:
- preliminary assessment;
- what can already be concluded;
- exactly what information is still needed;
- what the user should avoid doing before clarification.

## Urgency detection

Treat as urgent when there may be:
- imminent limitation/appeal/procedural deadlines;
- enforcement action;
- seizure/freezing/eviction;
- detention/criminal process;
- termination or dismissal with short challenge periods;
- active court hearing;
- risk of evidence disappearing.

Urgent matters should be escalated for human review.
