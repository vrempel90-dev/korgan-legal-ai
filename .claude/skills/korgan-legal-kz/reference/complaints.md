# Complaints / Жалобы

## Goal

Draft complaints to the correct authority with correct subject matter, evidence and requested action.

## Intake

Identify:
- complainant;
- person/body complained about;
- challenged act/omission;
- date;
- authority with competence;
- prior complaint/response;
- deadline;
- evidence;
- desired remedy.

## Competence gate

Before naming an authority:
- verify that the authority is competent;
- verify whether another body/court is the correct route;
- do not recommend prosecutor/inspectorate by habit.

## Structure

1. Addressee
2. Complainant details
3. Title
4. Facts
5. Challenged action/inaction
6. Verified legal basis
7. Why rights/interests are affected
8. Specific requests
9. Attachments
10. Date/signature

## Requests

Use specific requests:
- conduct review/inspection where legally available;
- cancel/reconsider/issue decision where legally available;
- provide response/information;
- take measures within competence.

Avoid vague or impossible demands.
