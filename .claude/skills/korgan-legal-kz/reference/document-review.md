# Document Review

## Review objective

Identify legal, commercial, procedural, and drafting risks in a user-provided document.

## Review sequence

1. Identify document type and purpose.
2. Identify parties and their roles.
3. Extract key obligations.
4. Extract money, deadlines, acceptance, renewal, termination, liability, dispute, confidentiality, data, and IP terms.
5. Identify one-sided or ambiguous clauses.
6. Compare key clauses against verified legal context when supplied.
7. Prioritize risks.
8. Suggest concrete amendments.

## Risk levels

Use:
- **Critical** — could create major loss, unenforceability, severe liability, missed procedural right, or immediate legal exposure.
- **High** — materially unfavorable or likely to cause dispute.
- **Medium** — ambiguity or imbalance that should be negotiated.
- **Low** — drafting, clarity, or administrative issue.

## Output

For each material issue provide:
- clause/section;
- risk level;
- what the clause does;
- why it matters;
- legal issue if verified;
- recommended change;
- proposed replacement wording when useful.

## Missing pages or unreadable content

Do not pretend the document is complete.
State which sections/pages are missing or unclear and limit conclusions accordingly.

## Signature and authority

When relevant, check:
- signatory identity;
- authority/power of attorney;
- party details;
- execution blocks;
- annexes referred to but missing.

## No silent rewriting

Do not rewrite the entire contract unless asked. First explain key risks and propose targeted changes.
