# KORGAN payment gate client feedback — 2026-08-20

Critical acceptance criteria:
- no generated Word document is delivered before payment verification;
- client must press payment flow, upload receipt, receipt must pass automated pre-check, and an administrator must manually confirm the actual Kaspi payment;
- rejected/missing/invalid receipt never unlocks a document;
- response-to-claim flow must not suggest generating a claim as the default next step;
- payment protection applies to claim, pretrial claim, response to claim, and contract;
- lawyer review CTA is shown only after the paid document has actually been released, and clearly states that review/additional documents are separate paid services.
