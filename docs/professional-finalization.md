# Professional claim finalization

The filing-facing claim is not trusted merely because a drafting model produced it.

After the latency-bounded research/draft/optional-repair path, KORGAN applies a zero-model-call finalization layer that:

- resolves a court only from source-bound venue law plus a versioned official court-directory cache;
- reconstructs the legal basis from accepted source-bound VERIFIED propositions so concrete articles cannot silently disappear;
- removes emotional/medical consequences and moral-damage relief when the user did not provide those facts;
- removes unsolicited evidentiary/procedural motions;
- excludes termination/rescission relief unless professional research explicitly marked it INCLUDE;
- recalculates the claim price after relief is removed;
- reruns deterministic state-duty and release checks.

The court directory is provenance-bearing data, not a legal inference. Entries are extended only from official court/government sources. Unknown locations remain fail-closed rather than guessed.
